    The short version

    This website sets no cookies of its own. It runs no analytics, no advertising or retargeting pixels, no session recording, no tag manager, no chat widget and no social media embeds. Fonts are served from our own servers rather than from a third-party font network.

    The only cookie you may encounter is a strictly necessary one set by the security service that sits in front of this site, described below.

    What this site sets

    Category Used here? Notes

    Advertising and retargetingNoNo advertising pixels of any kind.

    The one infrastructure cookie

    This site is served through a content-delivery and security network. To tell automated traffic apart from real visitors, that provider may set a short-lived cookie on your device — typically named __cf_bm and expiring after about 30 minutes.

    It exists to protect the site from bots and abuse. It does not identify you, is not used for advertising, and is not used to track you across other websites. Under the ePrivacy rules that apply in the UK and EEA, a cookie of this kind is treated as strictly necessary and does not require consent — but it does require disclosure, which is the purpose of this page.

    Local storage and similar technologies

    We do not use localStorage, sessionStorage, IndexedDB, web beacons, tracking pixels or device fingerprinting on this website.

    Why there is no cookie banner

    Consent banners exist because most sites load trackers that legally require your permission before they run. This one does not load any. Since the only cookie involved is strictly necessary for security, there is nothing for you to accept or reject, and a banner asking you to consent to nothing would be noise rather than a protection.

    This is a deliberate design decision, not an oversight. If we ever add analytics or any other non-essential technology, we will implement a proper consent mechanism that blocks it until you opt in, and we will update this page first.

    How to control cookies anyway

    Every major browser lets you view, block and delete cookies through its settings, usually under Privacy or Site Settings. Blocking the strictly necessary cookie described above may mean the security layer challenges your requests more often, but this site will continue to work.

    The HubSign application

    The HubSign application at {{ company.app_domain }} is a separate service and does use cookies — at minimum to keep you signed in and to protect forms against cross-site request forgery. Those are described in that service’s own documentation and in the agreement covering your account, not here.

    If this changes

    Any change to what this site loads will be reflected here, and the date at the top of the page will change with it. See also our Privacy Policy.
