    Status of this policy

    Our commercial refund terms are being finalised with legal counsel and are not published yet. We would rather say that plainly than publish terms we might have to change.

    Until they are published, refund requests are handled case by case — write to {{ company.sales_email }} and we will tell you where you stand before you commit to anything. Whatever we publish will not reduce the statutory rights described in Rights you have regardless, because those cannot be contracted away.

    The terms that actually bind us are the ones in the subscription agreement you accept when you create an account. If this page and that agreement ever disagree, that agreement governs — and if this page is more generous, we will honour this page.

    How to cancel

    You can cancel a HubSign subscription at any time from your account settings in the application at {{ company.app_domain }}. You do not have to call or email us to cancel, and we do not put a retention step in the way.

    On cancellation your plan stays active until the end of the period you have already paid for. After that the subscription does not renew.

    Billing and renewal

    • Monthly plans are charged monthly in advance and renew automatically until cancelled.
    • Annual plans are charged once, in advance, for twelve months, and renew automatically until cancelled.
    • Prices are shown in United States dollars and exclude applicable taxes, which are added at checkout.
    • Annual prices are displayed on our pricing page as a monthly-equivalent figure; the amount charged is that figure multiplied by twelve.

    We will send a renewal reminder before an annual plan renews, with a link that lets you cancel in one step.

    Rights you have regardless

    These apply whatever our commercial policy ends up saying. They are set out here so you can rely on them now.

    If you are a consumer in Ontario

    Ontario’s consumer protection legislation regulates automatic renewals, advance notice of renewal, and cancellation rights for consumer agreements, and gives you remedies for unfair practices. Those rights apply to consumer purchases regardless of what any contractual term says.

    If you are a consumer in Quebec

    Quebec’s Consumer Protection Act is stricter again, particularly around contracts entered into at a distance, mandatory disclosure before the contract is formed, and cancellation. Those rules cannot be overridden by contract, and this policy does not attempt to.

    If you are a consumer in the United Kingdom or the EEA

    You generally have a statutory right to cancel a distance contract within 14 days without giving a reason. Where you ask for the service to start immediately within that period, you may be charged for what you have actually used up to the point you cancel. We will not ask you to waive this right.

    If you are a consumer in Jamaica

    Jamaica’s Consumer Protection Act applies to our operation in that region, including its provisions on unfair contract terms and on the supply of services.

    Business customers

    Consumer legislation generally does not apply where you buy on behalf of an organization. Your rights are those in your subscription agreement.

    Getting your data out

    Whatever happens commercially, you can export your documents and records from HubSign before your access ends, and we will not hold your data hostage over a billing dispute. If you need help exporting, contact {{ company.support_email }} and we will assist.

    How to ask about a refund

    Email {{ company.sales_email }} with the account name and what you would like to happen. Tell us if you are asking as a consumer rather than on behalf of an organization, and where you are resident, since that determines which of the rights above apply.

    If we cannot resolve it between us, a consumer may be able to complain to the consumer protection regulator in their jurisdiction.
