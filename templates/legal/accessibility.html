    Our commitment

    {{ company.legal_name }} wants this website to be usable by as many people as possible, including people who navigate by keyboard, use a screen reader or magnifier, need larger text or higher contrast, or prefer reduced motion.

    We treat accessibility as part of building the site, not as a later pass. Where we fall short we would like to know, and we will fix it.

    The standard we aim for

    This website targets WCAG 2.1 Level AA, and we have also adopted the target-size and focus-appearance criteria added in WCAG 2.2.

    As an organization based in {{ company.jurisdiction }}, we are subject to the Accessibility for Ontarians with Disabilities Act (AODA). Its web accessibility requirement applies to larger private organizations; we aim to meet the standard whether or not the threshold applies to us, because the point is that people can use the site.

    What we have done

    • A “skip to main content” link is the first thing a keyboard user reaches.
    • Every control is a real button or link, reachable and operable by keyboard, with a visible focus indicator.
    • Expandable and selected states are exposed through ARIA, not colour alone, so screen readers report them.
    • The carousel announces each change through a live region, and supports arrow-key navigation.
    • Text meets or exceeds a 4.5:1 contrast ratio, and meaningful graphics 3:1. This is verified by an automated test that runs against our colour tokens, so a future change cannot quietly reintroduce a failure.
    • Interactive targets are at least 24×24 CSS pixels.
    • Headings run in order with a single page title, and the page uses proper landmark regions.
    • Decorative illustrations are hidden from assistive technology, so nothing meaningless is read aloud.
    • Nothing moves, flashes or auto-advances on its own. If your system asks for reduced motion, animation and smooth scrolling are switched off.
    • Content reflows to 320px without horizontal scrolling, and remains usable at 200% zoom.

    Known limitations

    We would rather list these than imply the site is perfect.

    • The carousel needs JavaScript. With JavaScript disabled the “One invoice, nine screens” section shows only the first screen. Every screen is illustrative, and no information appears there that is not also described in text elsewhere on the page.
    • No formal third-party audit yet. Our conformance claim is based on our own testing and automated checks, not an independent evaluation. We have not yet completed testing with every screen reader and browser combination in common use.
    • The product mockups are decorative. They are deliberately hidden from screen readers rather than described, because they are stylised geometry rather than real screenshots. If you would find described screenshots useful, please tell us.
