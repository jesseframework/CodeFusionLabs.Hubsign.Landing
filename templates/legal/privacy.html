    Who this covers

    This policy explains how {{ company.legal_name }} handles personal information in connection with the HubSign marketing website at {{ company.site_domain }}.

    It does not cover the HubSign application at {{ company.app_domain }}. Documents, signatures, audit records and account data you place in the application are governed by the agreement you accept when you create an account, together with any data processing agreement between us and your organization. If you signed a document through HubSign because someone else sent it to you, the organization that sent it is the controller of that document, and you should direct requests about it to them.

    {{ company.legal_name }} is incorporated in {{ company.jurisdiction }}, and is subject to Canada’s Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA). Depending on where you live, other laws may also apply to you — see Regional information.

    What this website collects

    This website is deliberately built to collect as little as possible. It has no contact form, no newsletter sign-up, no account area and no analytics. We do not build a profile of you, and we do not sell, rent or share personal information for advertising.

    What we do necessarily receive when you load a page:

    InformationWhyKept for
    Your IP address, browser type, requested page, referring page, and the time of the request Written to standard web-server logs. Needed to serve the page, to keep the service available, and to detect abuse such as denial-of-service traffic. A short operational period, then deleted or aggregated

    Fonts are served from our own servers rather than from a third-party font network, so loading this site does not disclose your IP address to a font provider.

    Cookies and tracking

    This website sets no cookies of its own and contains no analytics, advertising, session-replay or social-media tracking. There is nothing to consent to, which is why you are not shown a cookie banner.

    Our content-delivery and security provider may set a strictly necessary cookie to distinguish automated traffic from human visitors. It is used only for that purpose and not to track you across other websites. The Cookie Policy sets this out in full.

    When you contact us

    If you email {{ company.sales_email }} or {{ company.support_email }}, or call us, we receive whatever you choose to include — typically your name, email address, organization and the substance of your enquiry. We use it to answer you and to keep a record of the conversation.

    Please do not send sensitive personal information, or confidential documents, by unsolicited email.

    Why we may process information

    Under PIPEDA we rely on your consent, which is implied where the purpose is obvious from the circumstances — loading a web page necessarily discloses your IP address to the server that answers it, and emailing us necessarily discloses what you write. Where a purpose would not be obvious, we ask first.

    For visitors in the United Kingdom and the European Economic Area, our legal bases under the UK GDPR and the EU GDPR are:

    • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)) — serving the website securely, keeping server logs, and preventing abuse.
    • Legitimate interests — responding to an enquiry you send us.
    • Legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)) — where we are required to retain or disclose information by law.

    Who we share information with

    We do not sell personal information. We disclose it only:

    • to service providers who host, deliver and protect this website, acting on our instructions and bound to confidentiality;
    • where required by law, court order, or a lawful request from a public authority; and
    • to a successor entity in the event of a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, subject to this policy continuing to apply.

    Our providers may process data outside {{ company.jurisdiction }}, including in the United States. Where that happens, information may be accessible to courts and authorities in those countries under their local law. We use contractual protections intended to keep the level of protection comparable to that in Canada.

    How long we keep it

    Server logs are kept only as long as needed for security and operational troubleshooting, then deleted or aggregated so they no longer identify anyone. Email correspondence is kept for as long as needed to deal with your enquiry and to maintain a reasonable business record, then deleted.

    How we protect it

    The site is served over HTTPS. We apply access controls, a restrictive content security policy, and rate limiting, and we keep our software patched. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security; if a breach creates a real risk of significant harm we will notify affected individuals and the relevant regulator as the law requires.

    Your rights

    Under PIPEDA you may:

    • ask whether we hold personal information about you, and request access to it;
    • ask us to correct information that is inaccurate or incomplete;
    • ask how your information has been used and to whom it has been disclosed;
    • withdraw consent, subject to legal and contractual restrictions and reasonable notice; and
    • complain to us, and then to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada if you are not satisfied.

    We respond to access requests within 30 days where we can, and will tell you if we need longer. We do not charge for a routine request. We may need to verify your identity before acting, and we will only ask for what is necessary to do so.

    Regional information

    Quebec

    If you are in Quebec, Law 25 (the Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector, as amended) gives you additional rights, including the right to request that information be de-indexed or deleted in defined circumstances, and, for computerised information collected from you, the right to receive it in a structured, commonly used technological format. Our Privacy Officer is the person responsible for the protection of personal information for these purposes. You may complain to the Commission d’accès à l’information du Québec.

    United Kingdom and European Economic Area

    You have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection, and the right not to be subject to solely automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects — we make no such decisions on this website. You may complain to your national supervisory authority, or to the Information Commissioner’s Office in the UK.

    The European Commission recognises Canada as providing an adequate level of protection for personal information transferred to organisations subject to PIPEDA, so transfers to us do not require additional safeguards on that basis.

    Jamaica

    If you are in Jamaica, the Data Protection Act, 2020 applies to our processing in that jurisdiction, including rights of access and correction and the right to complain to the Office of the Information Commissioner.

    United States

    Some US states give residents rights to know, delete, correct and opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising, and this website runs no advertising trackers. You may still exercise your access and deletion rights by contacting us.

    Children

    HubSign is a business product. This website is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact our Privacy Officer and we will delete it.

    Changes to this policy

    We update this policy when our practices change. The date at the top shows when it last changed. Material changes will be signposted on this page.
